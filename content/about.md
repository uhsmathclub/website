# What We Do...

## Weekly Meetings

Math Club meets every Friday at lunch in Room 303. During these meetings, we typically give lectures about either competition math or an interesting topic in math, but will sometimes do other activities such as playing math games, doing math puzzles, or giving math homework help.

## Competitions

Math Club also participates in many math competitions. Some of these, such as the AMC and Math Madness, are very accessible and don't require lots of knowledge of competition style math, while others, like the Caltech Math Meet and the Stanford Math Tournament, are more advanced.

Math Club offers the AMC 10/12 in November at a significantly reduced price. Our lectures about competition math will mainly be in preparation for these AMC/AIME style contests.

## Volunteering

A group of UHS Math Club students volunteer to teach the Rancho San Joaquin MATHCOUNTS team! Email [uhsmathclub@gmail.com](mailto:uhsmathclub@gmail.com) for more information!
